# Rejected figures

A figure was used only if it passed three checks: a legend that assigns
each curve to a treatment, readable axes, and a clear treatment start.
Figures were rejected when curves could not be assigned, when the legend
was missing, when error bars overlapped so far that points could not be
read, or when no untreated control arm existed, because growth and kill
cannot be separated without one. Rejected figures were replaced, not
repaired. No rejected figure's image is included here; only the reason
is recorded.

- **Cisplatin in A549, first paper chosen.** The cisplatin arm existed
  only as endpoint values, not as a time course. Replaced by Li et al.
  2025, Table 1, which reports full values.
- **Doxorubicin in 4T1.** Seven overlapping curves and no usable
  legend. Replaced by Wang et al. 2019, Figure 4A (MCF-7).
- **Erlotinib in HCC827.** Treated arms only, no control arm.
  Replaced by Lu et al. 2008, Figure 3A (BxPC-3).
- **Docetaxel in PEAK1 cells.** No clean parental control-versus-drug
  curve. Replaced by Wang et al. 2016, Figure 1A (PC-3).
- **Gefitinib in PC-9, parental curve.** Not found after repeated
  searches. Dropped rather than filled with weak data.
- **Epirubicin in MCF-7.** Not found after repeated searches. Dropped
  rather than filled with weak data.
- **Epirubicin 9 mg/kg panel (Gubernator et al. 2014).** Rejected:
  free epirubicin at that dose killed 5 of 8 mice by day 16, so the
  curve is survivor-biased. Only the 6 mg/kg panel was used.
- **Gefitinib in HCC827, Mock arm (Okamoto et al. 2010, Figure 4A).**
  Low resolution, control arm ends early, treated arm sits at the
  floor. Failed the filter during the later batch hunt; not used.
- **Everolimus in MCF-7 (retracted paper).** Not used: the paper was
  retracted. Everolimus enters only through Yunokawa et al. 2012,
  Figure 5A (MDA-MB-468), which is outside the paper's 30 rows.
- **Vinorelbine, topotecan, olaparib, etoposide (later batch hunt).**
  No clean single-agent caliper time series with a control arm was
  found for any of them. Parked, not forced in.
